2025Preprint

Nonparametric Regression for Random Unbiased Perturbations ↗

Anna Lyubarskaja, Dominik Rothenhäusler

How random changes in a data-generating distribution alter prediction accuracy and the choice of model complexity.

arXiv ↗ PDF ↗
Overview +

Even when shifts introduce no systematic bias, they can make predictions less precise. We characterize this additional uncertainty through an effective sample size, derive implications for smoothing and model selection, and establish corresponding lower bounds.

2024Updated 2026

Data-driven Error Estimation: Excess Risk Bounds without Class Complexity as Input ↗

Sanath Kumar Krishnamurthy, Anna Lyubarskaja, Emma Brunskill, Susan Athey

Using the data to bound estimation error simultaneously across a collection of estimates.

arXiv ↗ PDF ↗
Overview +

We develop a way to turn guarantees for individual estimates into guarantees that hold across an entire collection. The approach learns from dependencies among errors and applies to confidence intervals, prediction guarantees, and exploration in contextual bandits.
